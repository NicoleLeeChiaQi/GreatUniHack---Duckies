What your score means

PASSPOTTY TRUST MODEL

On-time payments raise your score. Late or missed payments lower it. Your score helps determine which turn positions you can safely access.

A Trust Score is an internal PassPotty reliability measure. It is not a credit score, does not come from a credit reference agency and should not be represented as one.

Protection built into every group

⌑

Hold-back

Early-turn members receive the pot minus one month's contribution. It is released once all their payments are complete.

♧

Guarantor

A trusted member can vouch for someone and cover them if they stop paying, subject to the group agreement.

✳

Protection pool

The small extra paid by early turns helps cover missed payments so later members can still receive their full pot.

⌁

Earned early turns

New members start with later turns. Earlier turns are earned through a stronger Trust Score.

⚑

Order check

PassPotty flags an early placement for a low-score member and offers a later turn or guarantor.

▤

Group agreement

Every member accepts the amount, dates, turn and missed-payment rules before the group starts.
